title: Digital Root
difficulty: easy
tags: math, strings

Replace a number with the sum of its digits, and repeat until a single digit remains. That digit is the number's digital root.

For example, 9875 becomes 9 + 8 + 7 + 5 = 29, then 2 + 9 = 11, then 1 + 1 = 2.

## Input
The first line contains `t` (1 ≤ t ≤ 1000). Each of the next `t` lines contains a non-negative integer with at most 100 digits.

## Output
For each number, print its digital root on its own line.
