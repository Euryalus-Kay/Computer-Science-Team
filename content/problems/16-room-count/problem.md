title: Room Count
difficulty: medium
tags: sorting, greedy

Each meeting runs from minute `s` up to, but not including, minute `e`. A room can host one meeting at a time, and a meeting ending at minute 30 frees its room for a meeting starting at minute 30.

Find the fewest rooms needed to hold every meeting.

## Input
The first line contains `n` (1 ≤ n ≤ 6 · 10⁴). Each of the next `n` lines contains `s` and `e` (0 ≤ s < e ≤ 10⁹).

## Output
Print the minimum number of rooms.
