---
title: Mirror Sentence
difficulty: easy
tags: strings
---
A sentence is a **mirror sentence** if its letters read the same forwards and backwards. Ignore case and everything that is not a letter.

`Was it a car or a cat I saw?` is a mirror sentence.

## Input
The first line contains `t` (1 ≤ t ≤ 100). Each of the next `t` lines is a sentence of at most 1000 characters with at least one letter.

## Output
For each sentence print `YES` or `NO`.
